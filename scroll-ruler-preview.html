🔬 Misconception Buster
Inertia & Gravitational Field

THE HEAVY FALL MYTH

Do heavy objects fall faster? No! While gravity exerts a stronger pull on larger mass, its proportional inertia precisely balances the acceleration: a = F / m. In a vacuum, all objects fall together at g = 9.8 m/s².
