🔬 Misconception Buster
Gravity & Vacuum Drop

THE HEAVY FALL MYTH

Do heavy objects fall faster? No! The greater gravitational force on a heavier mass is perfectly balanced by its greater inertia resisting acceleration (a = F / m). In a vacuum, a hammer and feather fall at identical rates (g = 9.8 m/s²).
