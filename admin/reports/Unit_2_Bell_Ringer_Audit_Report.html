Diagnostic Spotlight: Dimensional Analysis & The 231,200 Error
The "Multiply Instead of Divide" Reflex (14%):
On Day 10 Part 2, 16 out of 114 students multiplied 680 m · 340 m/s, entering 231,200 instead of dividing to get 2.0 seconds.
Key Finding: Students who made this error immediately recognized in Part 3 that multiplying meters by meters/second creates m²/s rather than time (seconds).
The Desmos Calculator Integration:
Adding the pop-out official CAST Desmos Scientific Calculator alongside the phenomenon data allowed 69% of students to compute and copy accurate decimal figures directly into the test fields without switching browser tabs.
EXEMPLAR CAST DEFENSE: Dimensional Analysis Elizabeth Stewart (Period 5 CP Physics)
AI Mentor: You found that the Scenario C ultrasonic pulse takes 2.0 seconds! In your own words, how does checking the units of your givens help you decide whether to multiply or divide? What would happen if you accidentally multiplied distance by speed?
Elizabeth: Checking the units helps me know whether to multiply or divide. Since meters need to cancel out and leave seconds, I divide distance by speed. If I multiplied them, the units would be meters²/second (m²/s), which is not a unit of time.
AI Mentor: You nailed it! Thinking about how labels combine is like an automatic error-detector. If your math spits out m²/s when you're looking for seconds, you know right away to flip the operation!
