Unit Overview

This unit establishes the mathematical and physical foundations of motion. Students analyze 1D Kinematics by investigating how displacement, time, velocity, and acceleration interlock. Through hands-on experiments—including "The Monster's Journey" lab and incline ramp timing—students learn to rigorously differentiate between scalar speed (v = Δx / Δt) and directional vector velocity (v = Δx / Δt), decode position-time (x-t) and velocity-time (v-t) coordinate slopes, and model multi-stage velocity systems.

Cognitive Learning Objectives

DOK 1: Recall & Formula

Define distance, displacement, time, and calculate speed using algebraic substitution (v = x / t).

DOK 2: Skills & Concepts

Construct distance-time graphs, calculate coordinate slopes, and differentiate scalar speed from directional velocity vectors.

DOK 3: Strategic Thinking

Design and execute ramp incline experiments, decode non-linear motion graphs, and solve multi-stage intercept problems.

DOK 4: Extended Modeling

Synthesize kinematic models, evaluate error propagation in stopwatch timing, and defend claims on vehicle motion systems.
