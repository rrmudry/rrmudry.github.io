Unit Driving Inquiry

Four Essential Questions

EQ1 • Work & Power (Week 1)

Why does carrying a heavy backpack across a flat hallway require stamina yet count as zero mechanical work in physics, while running up stairs takes the same work yet demands more power?

EQ2 • Stored Energy (Week 2)

How do vertical height, spring stiffness (k), and stretch distance store measurable capacity to launch objects or cause motion?

EQ3 • Speed & Braking Distance (Week 3)

Why does doubling a car's speed require four times the braking distance instead of twice the braking distance?

EQ4 • Conservation Accounting (Week 4)

How can an energy ledger (PEi + KEi + Wext = PEf + KEf) predict speeds and maximum hill heights on any coaster without tracking curve forces?

By the End of Unit 3

What You Will Be Able to Do

  • Work: Tell when a force does work, and find the work from a force and a distance.
  • Power: Measure your own power climbing stairs, in watts.
  • Stored energy: Measure how a spring stretches and how height stores energy.
  • Speed and energy: Use a photogate (a light beam that times an object passing through it) to find how energy changes with speed.
  • Energy bar charts: Track energy from the top of a coaster hill to the bottom, and design a marble coaster that works.

Energy & Work Formula Matrix

🎢 Coaster Physics App ↗ ⚙️ Pulley & Work App ↗ ⚙️📐 Pulleys & Ramps Lab ↗
1. Mechanical Work
W = F · d  (or Area under F–d graph)

Units: Joules (J = N·m). Zero when F ⟂ d. Negative when friction or braking opposes motion. Angled pull: W = F · d · cos θ.

2. Mechanical Power
P = Wt   (or P = F · v)

Units: Watts (W = J/s). Rate of energy transfer per second (746 W = 1 hp).

3. Gravitational Potential Energy
PEg = m · g · h

Units: Joules (J). Always use g = 10 m/s². Only height change Δh changes ΔPEg.

4. Hooke's Law & Spring Energy
Fs = k · x  |  PEs = 12 · k · x²

Units: k in N/m, x = L − L0 in m. Doubling spring stretch x quadruples stored energy!

5. Kinetic Energy & Speed
KE = 12 · m · v²

Units: Joules (J). Doubling speed quadruples KE and braking distance.

6. Work–Energy & Conservation
Wnet = KEf − KEi
PEi + KEi + Wf = PEf + KEf

Friction does negative work, Wf = −Ff · d, which removes mechanical energy.
