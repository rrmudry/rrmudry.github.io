Mechanical Work Practice

1. How much work does a student do if they push a lab cart forward with a force of 45 N for 6 m? 2. The brakes on a car do 3,000 J of work over a distance of 25 m to slow it down. How much force are the brakes putting on the car? 3. A warehouse forklift pushes a crate with a force of 150 N and does 900 J of work. How many meters did the crate move?
4. A dog pulls a sled across the snow with a horizontal force of 80 N for 35 m. How much work does the dog do? 5. A tugboat pulls a barge with a constant force of 4,000 N and does 100,000 J of work. How far did the tugboat pull the barge? 6. A baseball player slides 4 m into second base while dirt friction does 600 J of work to stop them. How much friction force acted on the player?
7. How much work is required to lift a 12 kg box of books straight up onto a shelf that is 2 m above the floor? 8. A person loads a 25 kg crate of oranges onto the back of a truck and does 375 J of work. How high is the back of the truck? 9. A crane does 18,000 J of work lifting a steel beam straight up to a roof 15 m above the ground. What is the mass of the steel beam?
10. Two students push a heavy crate 10 m across a floor with a forward force of 120 N while friction pushes backward with 40 N. What is the net work done on the crate? 11. A weightlifter lifts a 60 kg barbell straight up 1.5 m, holds it still overhead for 4 seconds, and then lifts it another 0.5 m higher. How much total lifting work did the weightlifter do? 12. A person pushes against a locked steel door with a force of 400 N for 20 minutes, but the door does not move. How much work did the person do?
