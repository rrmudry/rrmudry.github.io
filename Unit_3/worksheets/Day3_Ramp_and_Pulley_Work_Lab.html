Target: Raise 0.50 kg Cart to Fixed Shelf Height h = 0.30 m
Investigative Question: How does changing ramp pull distance d affect required effort force Fpull and total mechanical work Win when every path reaches the same shelf height (h = 0.30 m)?
  1. Calibrate: Zero your 0–10 N spring scale vertically, hang the 0.50 kg cart, and confirm weight Fg = m · g = 5.0 N (g = 10 m/s²).
  2. Verify Output Work: Lifting 0.30 m straight up requires ideal output work Wout = m · g · h = 1.50 J on all trials.
  3. Ramp Pulls: Set ramp tracks to d = 0.50, 0.60, 1.00, and 1.20 m terminating at h = 0.30 m. Pull strictly parallel at a slow, steady speed (no jerks). Average 2 pulls per ramp.
