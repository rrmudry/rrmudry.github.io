🎢 Engineering Constraints & Deliverables
All 3 Must Pass Live Verification
Requirement 1
Hill > 20 cm

Your initial drop hill must have a vertical height difference of at least 20.0 cm measured with a ruler from peak to valley.

Requirement 2
Vertical Loop

The marble must complete a full 360° vertical loop without derailing or losing track contact. Velocity at the top must stay high enough so gravity does not pull it off!

Requirement 3
Finish ≥ 20 cm

Your coaster must rise back up to finish at least 20.0 cm above the lowest point on the track! Proves your team preserved mechanical energy against friction.
