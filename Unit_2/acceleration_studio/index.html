🔑 The Golden Rule of Acceleration Signs:

• Matching Signs (both + or both -): The object is SPEEDING UP.
• Opposite Signs (+ / - or - / +): The object is SLOWING DOWN.
• Negative acceleration does NOT mean slowing down—it only means the rate of change is directed toward the negative direction!
