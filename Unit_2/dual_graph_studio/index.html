📖 Kinematics Dual-Graph Quick Reference

Position vs. Time (x-t)

  • Vertical Axis: Position in meters (where the car is).
  • Horizontal Axis: Time elapsed in seconds.
  • Slope: Equals Velocity (v = Δx ÷ Δt).
  • Slanted Up: Moving forward.
  • Flat Horizontal: Stopped (velocity = 0 m/s).
  • Slanted Down: Moving backward in reverse toward 0m.

Velocity vs. Time (v-t)

  • Vertical Axis: Velocity in m/s (speed and direction).
  • Horizontal Axis: Time elapsed in seconds.
  • Horizontal Flat Line: Moving at steady constant speed!
  • Line ON 0 m/s Axis: Car is STOPPED.
  • Above 0 m/s Axis: Moving forward (+).
  • Below 0 m/s Axis: Moving backward in reverse (-).
