📖 How to Read Velocity vs. Time (v-t) Graphs

1. Value vs. Slope

  • Value of the curve: The height on the vertical axis tells you the object's instantaneous velocity (speed and direction) in m/s.
  • Slope of the curve: The steepness equals acceleration (a = Δv / Δt) in m/s².

2. Flat Line vs. Uphill/Downhill Slope

  • Flat horizontal line: Constant speed! Acceleration is 0 m/s². The car is NOT stopped unless it sits on 0 m/s.
  • Uphill slope (positive slope): Constant positive acceleration. Velocity is increasing.
  • Downhill slope (negative slope): Constant negative acceleration. Velocity is decreasing.

3. Area Under the Curve (Displacement)

The geometric area bounded between the velocity line and the horizontal zero axis equals displacement:

  • Area above axis (v > 0): Positive forward displacement (+Δx).
  • Area below axis (v < 0): Negative backward displacement (-Δx).
