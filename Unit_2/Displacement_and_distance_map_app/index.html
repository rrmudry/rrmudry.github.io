Cartographer Mission: Charting Distance vs. Displacement

Measure the winding trail length (scalar distance) with a flexible string, then draw the straight vector arrow (net displacement) from the origin!

1. Scalar Distance (d)

The total ground length traveled along the actual path. Because trails curve around obstacles, mountains, and swamps, distance accumulates with every step. Distance is always a positive scalar.

2. Vector Displacement (Δr)

The straight-line vector arrow connecting the Initial Position (Origin A) directly to the Final Position (Destination D): Δr = r_final - r_initial. Has both magnitude and direction!

3. The Homecoming Paradox

When the traveler returns home along the same path: Total Distance doubles (2 × d), but Net Displacement becomes exactly 0 because final position equals initial position!
