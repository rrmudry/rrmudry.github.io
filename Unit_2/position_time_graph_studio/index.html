Where is the Car?

The side axis (up/down) tells you where the car is on the ground in meters. The bottom axis tells you the clock time in seconds.

🏔️

Steep vs. Flat

A steep line (like a mountain cliff) means the car is moving fast. A gentle line means it is moving slow. A flat line means it is parked/stopped.

📐

Rise over Run

Count how many meters it went UP (Rise), and divide by how many seconds it went ACROSS (Run).
Speed = Rise ÷ Run.

🔙

Going Backwards

When a line slopes downward, the car is driving in reverse back toward the start. Its direction is negative, but its speed is always positive.

📊

Reading a Data Table

Each point on a graph is written as (Time, Position). For example, at 3 seconds, the car is at 6 meters: (3s, 6m).

✍️

Plotting Points

Find the time on the bottom, go straight up to the position number, click to place a dot, and connect the dots with straight lines!
