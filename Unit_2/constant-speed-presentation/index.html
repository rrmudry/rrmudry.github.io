Foundational Concept

What Exactly IS Speed?

  • Scalar Quantity: Speed describes how fast an object moves — direction does not matter.
  • Rate of Travel: The distance covered in one single unit of time (e.g., every 1 second or 1 hour).
  • Constant Speed: Covering equal distances in equal intervals of time — zero acceleration.

Analogy: The Ticking Meter Stick

If a wind-up beetle moves at a constant 5 cm/s:

Second 1 → Moves 5 cm
Second 2 → Moves another 5 cm (10 cm total)
Second 3 → Moves another 5 cm (15 cm total)

Never speeds up. Never slows down. Churns out 5 cm during every second on the clock!

Formula 1 of 3

Calculating Speed: v = d / t

When you know Distance and Time:
v = d / t
Speed = Distance ÷ Time

Standard Units in Motion

m/s (Meters per Second) Standard SI unit for physics laboratory problems.
cm/s (Centimeters per Second) Used for tabletop inquiry (like our 20.0 cm wind-up toys!).
km/h or mph Used for road vehicles and long-distance navigation.
The Algebraic Wheel

The "Magic" Formula Triangle

  • Distance (d) sits on top (the numerator).
  • Speed (v) and Time (t) share the bottom.
  • The horizontal bar means DIVIDE (÷).
  • The vertical bar means MULTIPLY (·).
💡 The Thumb Rule: Cover the variable you need to find. The remaining letters give you the exact equation!
Formula 2 of 3

Solving for Distance: d = v · t

When you know Speed and Time:
d = v · t
Distance = Speed × Time

Worked Example: The Toy Beetle

A wind-up beetle walks at a steady speed of 4.5 cm/s. How far does it travel in 6.0 seconds?

Given: v = 4.5 cm/s, t = 6.0 s
Formula: d = v · t
Substitute: d = (4.5 cm/s) · (6.0 s)
Answer: d = 27.0 cm

Notice seconds (s) cancel out, leaving centimeters (cm)!

Formula 3 of 3

Solving for Time: t = d / v

When you know Distance and Speed:
t = d / v
Time = Distance ÷ Speed

Worked Example: Crossing the Classroom

A motorized buggy travels at 0.25 m/s. How much time does it need to cross a 3.0 meter lab track?

Given: d = 3.0 m, v = 0.25 m/s
Formula: t = d / v
Substitute: t = 3.0 m / 0.25 m/s
Answer: t = 12.0 seconds

Mental Math Anchor: How many quarters ($0.25) are in $3.00? Exactly 12!

Key Takeaways & Today's Mission

The Constant Motion Triad

1. Speed: v = d / t (Divide distance by time)
2. Distance: d = v · t (Multiply speed by time)
3. Time: t = d / v (Divide distance by speed)
