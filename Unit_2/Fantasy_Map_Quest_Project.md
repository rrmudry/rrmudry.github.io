## ⏱️ The 3-Day Quest Pacing (45-Minute Class Periods)

Because careful cartography and accurate measurement take time, this project is paced across 3 focused work sessions:

* **Day 1: Realm Cartography & Scale Setup (~30 min work time)**
  * Establish origin point `(0, 0)`, draw the compass rose, choose your map scale factor, and start sketching terrain geography.
  * Begin placing and labeling the 4 required landmarks with coordinate points `(x, y)`.
* **Day 2: Realm Cartography & Geographic Detailing (~30 min work time)**
  * Continue drafting and detailing the realm map, color terrain hazards (forests, swamps, mountain passes), and finalize landmark coordinates.
  * Calibrate map grid scale keys and verify reference origins before plotting travel trails.
* **Day 3: The Journey Trail, String Technique (Distance vs. Displacement) & Analysis (~30 min work time)**
  * Finalize map drawings and plot the adventurer's winding quest trail connecting all 4 landmarks.
  * Use the string measurement technique along curves to measure paper centimeters and calculate total realm distance.
  * Draw the straight-line vector arrow from origin to destination to measure net displacement magnitude and direction.
  * Complete the project worksheet analysis questions.

## 🗺️ The Mission Briefing

Welcome to the cartography guild! You and your partner are royal mapmakers commissioned to chart a mysterious fantasy realm. An intrepid adventurer is about to set out from their humble starting village on a perilous quest to recover an ancient relic.

Your task is to:
1. **Design a rich fantasy map** with an established coordinate grid, origin point, scale factor, and compass rose.
2. **Chart the adventurer's winding journey** through swamps, mountains, forests, and ruins.
3. **Calculate the physical difference** between the total path distance the traveler walked versus their net displacement from home.

---

## 📐 Map Requirements

Your map must be drawn neatly and include the following elements:

1. **The Origin Point (0, 0):** Mark the traveler’s home village or starting tavern. This is your reference point for all measurements.
2. **Compass Rose:** Clearly mark North, South, East, and West in the corner of your map.
3. **The Grid System:** Use standard grid lines (e.g., 1 cm grid boxes).
4. **Map Scale Key:** Establish a clear conversion scale in the legend:
   * *Example:* **1.0 cm on paper = 10.0 leagues (or miles / km)**
5. **Four Required Quest Landmarks:**
   * **Landmark A:** The Starting Village / Origin `(0, 0)`
   * **Landmark B:** The First Obstacle (e.g., Whispering Forest, Misty Swamp)
   * **Landmark C:** The Waypoint (e.g., Mountain Pass, Wizard's Tower)
   * **Landmark D:** The Final Destination (e.g., Dragon's Lair, Sunken Citadel)
6. **The Meandering Trail:** Draw the path the traveler actually walks. Real trails curve around impassable mountains, follow winding rivers, and dodge monster territories—**do not draw a straight line!**

## 🧭 Data Collection & Measurements

### Step 1: Measuring Total Path Distance
*Because trails are winding, a straight ruler won't work!*  
1. Lay your piece of string carefully along the exact curves of the trail for each leg of the quest.
2. Pinch the string, lay it against a metric ruler, and record the length in centimeters.
3. Use your **Map Scale** to convert the centimeter measurement into actual realm units (leagues/miles/km).

| Quest Segment | Path on Paper (cm) | Scale Factor | Real World Distance (Leagues / Miles / km) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Leg 1:** Village (A) → Landmark (B) | ________ cm | 1 cm = ____ units | ________________ units |
| **Leg 2:** Landmark (B) → Landmark (C) | ________ cm | 1 cm = ____ units | ________________ units |
| **Leg 3:** Landmark (C) → Destination (D) | ________ cm | 1 cm = ____ units | ________________ units |
| **TOTAL DISTANCE TRAVELED (Leg 1 + 2 + 3):** | ________ cm | — | **________________ units** |

---

### Step 2: Measuring Net Displacement
*Displacement is a vector: it only cares about where you started and where you ended!*  
1. Place your ruler directly on the **Starting Village (A)** and draw a straight, colored arrow pointing directly to the **Final Destination (D)**.
2. Measure the length of this straight arrow in centimeters.
3. Convert the length into realm units using your scale.
4. Measure or estimate the compass direction of the arrow (e.g., *Northeast*, *30° North of East*).

* **Straight-Line Paper Measurement:** ____________ cm
* **Net Displacement Magnitude:** ____________ leagues / miles / km
* **Displacement Direction:** _________________________________________________
* **Final Vector Notation:** **Δr** = ____________ [units] in the direction of ____________

## 📜 The Quest Log: Post-Lab Analysis

Answer the following questions in complete sentences with your partner:

1. **Scalar vs. Vector:**  
   Compare your **Total Distance** value to your **Net Displacement** magnitude. Which value is larger, and why will distance *almost always* be greater than or equal to displacement?

2. **The Shortcut Scenario:**  
   Under what single, rare condition would a traveler's total distance traveled be *exactly equal* to their displacement?

3. **The Homecoming Paradox:**  
   After vanquishing the dragon at Landmark D, your victorious adventurer walks all the way back to the Starting Village (Landmark A) along the exact same path.
   * What is the traveler’s **new total distance traveled** for the round trip?  
     *Show calculation:*
   * What is the traveler’s **new net displacement** for the round trip? Explain why in terms of `Δx = x_final - x_initial`.

4. **Speed vs. Velocity Preview:**  
   If the traveler took 20 days of walking to complete the entire round trip back home, explain why their **average speed** was positive and active, while their **average velocity** for the trip was exactly 0.
