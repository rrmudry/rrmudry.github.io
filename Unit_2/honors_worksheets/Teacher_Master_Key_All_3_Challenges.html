Emergency Drone & Rescue Vessel Rendezvous (Delayed Intercept)
CONFIDENTIAL • MR. MUDRY
Part 1: Mathematical Kinematic DerivationsHS-PS2-1

Step 1A (Vessel Position Equation): Initial position x₀ = +300 m, constant speed v = +6.0 m/s.

x_ship(t) = 300 + 6.0 · t

Step 1B (Drone Position Equation for t ≥ 20 s): Launch position x₀ = 0 m, speed v = +18.0 m/s, delay = 20 s.

Factored: x_drone(t) = 18.0 · (t - 20)
Expanded: x_drone(t) = 18.0 · t - 360   (for t ≥ 20 s)

Step 1C (Algebraic Intercept Equating):

300 + 6.0 · t = 18.0 · t - 360
300 + 360 = 18.0 · t - 6.0 · t
660 = 12.0 · t
t = 660 / 12.0 = 55.0 seconds (Rendezvous Time)

x_meet = 300 + 6.0(55.0) = 300 + 330 = 630.0 meters from dock (Rendezvous Position)
Check with Drone: x_drone = 18.0(55 - 20) = 18.0(35) = 630.0 m ✓
Part 2: Completed Telemetry Table
Vehiclet = 0 st = 10 st = 20 st = 30 st = 40 st = 50 st = 55 s (Meet)t = 60 s
Ship x (m)300 m360 m420 m480 m540 m600 m630 m660 m
Drone x (m)0 m0 m0 m180 m360 m540 m630 m720 m
Part 4: Mission Engineering Audit & Constraint Solutions

1. Slope Verification: Ship slope = Rise/Run = (600 - 300)/(50 - 0) = +6.0 m/s. Drone slope = (540 - 0)/(50 - 20) = 540/30 = +18.0 m/s. The visual lines intersect exactly at coordinate (55 s, 630 m), verifying the algebra.

2. Flight Duration vs Clock Time: The mission clock reads t = 55 s, but the drone was delayed on dock until t = 20 s. Flight duration = 55 s - 20 s = 35.0 seconds of actual flight.

3. Battery Safety Audit: Total round-trip distance = 630 m (outbound) + 630 m (inbound) = 1,260 meters. Since certified battery limit is only 1,200 m, the drone cannot make it back to dock. It will exhaust power and ditch in the ocean 60 meters short of the harbor dock.

Tactical Highway Surveillance & Patrol Intercept (Piecewise Motion)
CONFIDENTIAL • MR. MUDRY
Part 1: Target Piecewise BreakdownHS-PS2-1

Leg 1 (0 to 20 s): v₁ = +15.0 m/s, Δt₁ = 20 s  →  Δx₁ = (15.0)(20) = +300 m. Coordinate: (20 s, 300 m)

Leg 2 (20 to 50 s): v₂ = 0.0 m/s, Δt₂ = 30 s  →  Δx₂ = 0 m. Coordinate: (50 s, 300 m) (Horizontal flat line)

Leg 3 (50 to 90 s): v₃ = +25.0 m/s, Δt₃ = 40 s  →  Δx₃ = (25.0)(40) = +1,000 m.
Final Position: x_final = 300 + 1,000 = 1,300 meters. Coordinate: (90 s, 1300 m)

Part 2: Cruiser Pursuit Strategies

Strategy Alpha (Catch at Rest Stop at t = 50 s):

Dispatch time: t = 30 s  |  Target arrival time: t = 50 s  →  Available travel time: Δt = 50 - 30 = 20.0 s
Required distance: Δx = 300 m
Required Cruiser Speed: v_alpha = 300 m / 20.0 s = 15.0 m/s (33.6 mph)

Strategy Beta (Catch at Highway End at t = 90 s):

Dispatch time: t = 30 s  |  Final mission time: t = 90 s  →  Available travel time: Δt = 90 - 30 = 60.0 s
Required distance: Δx = 1,300 m
Required Cruiser Speed: v_beta = 1,300 m / 60.0 s = 21.67 m/s ≈ 21.7 m/s (48.5 mph)
Part 4: Tactical Analysis & Speed Comparison

1. Horizontal Slope: On an x-t graph, slope = velocity (Δx/Δt). A flat horizontal line has slope = 0, proving instantly that position is unchanging and velocity is zero without needing telemetry.

2. Average Speed vs. Instantaneous Speeds:
Total distance = 1,300 m  |  Total time = 90.0 s  →  v_avg = 1,300 m / 90 s = 14.4 m/s.
Why lower than 15 m/s and 25 m/s? The 30 seconds of zero velocity at the rest stop significantly drags down the overall time-weighted average speed.

3. Speed Limit Constraint (Cap = 20.0 m/s):
Strategy Alpha requires 15.0 m/s ≤ 20.0 m/s (PHYSICALLY POSSIBLE ✓).
Strategy Beta requires 21.7 m/s > 20.0 m/s (EXCEEDS VEHICLE TOP SPEED ✗).
Therefore, Strategy Alpha is the only viable option for the patrol interceptor.

TEACHER MASTER KEY: CHALLENGE 3

Alpine Rescue Relay: Road Distance vs. Drone Displacement
CONFIDENTIAL • MR. MUDRY
Part 1: Vector Displacement vs. Scalar DistanceHS-PS2-1

Step 1A (Pythagorean Vector Magnitude): Δx = 800 m, Δy = 600 m.

Δr = √[(800)² + (600)²] = √[640,000 + 360,000] = √[1,000,000] = 1,000 meters

Step 1B (Comparison): Rover road distance = 1,800 m. Drone displacement = 1,000 m. The rover travels 800 m more because it is constrained to winding canyon switchbacks that deviate from the direct straight-line vector.

Part 2: Travel Time Calculations

Step 2A (Ground Rover Alpha): Road distance = 1,800 m, speed = 15.0 m/s.

t_rover = 1,800 m / 15.0 m/s = 120.0 seconds (2.0 minutes)

Step 2B (Rescue Drone Beta Timeline): Cruise speed = 20.0 m/s, flight distance = 1,000 m.

Leg 1 Flight (0 to 500 m): t₁ = 500 m / 20.0 m/s = 25.0 s (Clock: 0 to 25 s)
Thermal Cooldown Pause: Δt_pause = 35.0 s (Clock: 25 to 60 s, distance remains at 500 m)
Leg 2 Flight (500 to 1,000 m): t₂ = 500 m / 20.0 m/s = 25.0 s (Clock: 60 to 85 s)
Total Drone Arrival Time: t_drone = 25 + 35 + 25 = 85.0 seconds
Part 4: Mission Debrief & Race Analysis

1. Margin of Victory: Drone arrives at t = 85 s; Rover arrives at t = 120 s. Drone Beta wins by 35.0 seconds (120 s - 85 s).

2. Distance-Time Flat Line Interpretation: From t = 25 s to 60 s, the graph is horizontal at d = 500 m. The slope (speed) is 0 m/s. The odometer/distance accumulated stops increasing while the drone cools on the ridge.

3. Critical Breakdown Threshold (Honors Proof):
Rover total time = 120 s. Total drone flight time in the air = 25 s + 25 s = 50 s.
Maximum cooldown duration: Δt_max_pause = 120 s - 50 s = 70.0 seconds.
If the cooldown exceeds 70 seconds, the rover beats the drone to the climber.
