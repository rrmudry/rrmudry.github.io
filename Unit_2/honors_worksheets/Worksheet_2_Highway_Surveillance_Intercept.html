SURVEILLANCE DOSSIER: MULTI-STAGE RECONNAISSANCE TRACKING
Highway traffic sensors and an overhead observation satellite track a suspect vehicle heading eastbound on a straight desert highway starting from coordinate x = 0 m at t = 0 s. The target's journey consists of three distinct kinematic legs:
• Leg 1 (t = 0 to 20 s): Drives at a steady speed of 15.0 m/s.
• Leg 2 (t = 20 to 50 s): Pulls into an emergency turnout / rest stop and remains completely stationary (v = 0 m/s) for 30 seconds.
• Leg 3 (t = 50 to 90 s): Re-enters the highway and accelerates to a new uniform cruise speed of 25.0 m/s for 40 seconds.
A Highway Patrol Interceptor is stationed at coordinate x = 0 m. Due to dispatch verification, the cruiser cannot roll until t = 30 seconds. Once dispatched, it travels at a single uniform speed.
