EMERGENCY DISPATCH: ALPINE MOUNTAIN RELIEF OPERATION
A distress beacon is activated by an injured mountaineer located at coordinates (800 m East, 600 m North) from Base Camp (designated as origin 0, 0). Two emergency response units depart Base Camp at the exact same moment (t = 0 s):
• All-Terrain Ground Rover Alpha: Must follow a treacherous, winding switchback canyon road to avoid ravines. The total odometer road distance is d_road = 1,800 meters. The rover maintains a maximum steady speed of v_rover = 15.0 m/s along the road.
• Autonomous Sky-Crane Drone Beta: Flies straight as an arrow directly from Base Camp over the forest canopy to the climber's coordinates at a cruise speed of v_drone = 20.0 m/s. However, due to high altitude rotor heating, the drone must land on a midway ridge for a mandatory 35-second thermal cooldown pause (at the 500 m mark).
