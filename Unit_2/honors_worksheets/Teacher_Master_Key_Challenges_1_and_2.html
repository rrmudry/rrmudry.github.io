Challenge 1: Ship & Drone Kinematic RendezvousPage 1 of Student Sheet

Plotted Points on Grid:
• Ship: (0s, 300m), (10s, 360m), (20s, 420m), (30s, 480m), (40s, 540m), (50s, 600m), (55s, 630m), (60s, 660m).
• Drone: (0 to 20s flat on dock at 0m), (30s, 180m), (40s, 360m), (50s, 540m), (55s, 630m), (60s, 720m).

1. Rendezvous Coordinates: Time t = 55.0 seconds, Position x = 630.0 meters from dock.
2. Slopes (Speeds): Ship Slope = +6.0 m/s • Drone Slope (after t=20s) = (540 - 0)/(50 - 20) = +18.0 m/s.
3. Actual Drone Flight Duration: 55 s - 20 s delay = 35.0 seconds flying in the air.
4. Battery Safety Audit: Total round-trip distance = 630 m outbound + 630 m return = 1,260 meters.
→ Since battery max is 1,200 m, the drone cannot make it back (crashes 60 m short of the dock).
Challenge 2: Tactical Highway Surveillance InterceptPage 2 of Student Sheet

Plotted Points on Grid:
• Target Car: (0s, 0m) → (20s, 300m) [Leg 1] → (50s, 300m) [Leg 2, flat line] → (90s, 1300m) [Leg 3].
• Patrol Line A: Starts at (30s, 0m), connects to rest stop at (50s, 300m).
• Patrol Line B: Starts at (30s, 0m), connects to final highway point at (90s, 1300m).

1. Rest Stop Meaning: Slope is zero (Δx/Δt = 0), which means velocity is 0 m/s and position does not change (car is stationary).
2. Cruiser Speed Calculations:
• Strategy A (Rest Stop at t=50s): Slope = (300 m - 0 m) / (50 s - 30 s) = 300 / 20 = 15.0 m/s (33.6 mph).
• Strategy B (Highway End at t=90s): Slope = (1,300 m - 0 m) / (90 s - 30 s) = 1,300 / 60 = 21.67 m/s ≈ 21.7 m/s (48.5 mph).
3. Speed Limit Constraint (Cruiser Top Speed = 20.0 m/s):
• Strategy A requires 15.0 m/s ≤ 20.0 m/s → PHYSICALLY POSSIBLE ✓
• Strategy B requires 21.7 m/s > 20.0 m/s → EXCEEDS SPEED LIMIT / IMPOSSIBLE ✗
→ Therefore, Strategy A is the only viable option for the patrol interceptor.
