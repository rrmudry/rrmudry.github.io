📚 Mass vs. Weight vs. Volume: Three Different Things

Quantity What it is Unit Changes on the Moon or in space? Measured with Tells you about inertia?
Mass (m) The amount of matter in an object, and the measure of its inertia kg Never. 50 kg on Earth = 50 kg on the Moon = 50 kg in deep space Pan balance (on Earth), inertia balance (anywhere) YES. Mass IS the measure of inertia
Weight (W) The gravitational pull on an object: W = m · g N Yes. Earth 10, Moon 1.6, Mars 3.7, Jupiter 24.8, deep space ≈ 0 m/s² Spring scale or bathroom scale Only indirectly. On one planet more mass means more weight, but at 0 N the inertia is still all there
Volume (V) The 3D space an object takes up cm³ or L Never. (Gravity doesn't change an object's size) Ruler (L × W × H) or water displacement NO. A giant foam block has far less inertia than a small gold bar
