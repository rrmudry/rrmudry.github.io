📐 Step 1: Laboratory Apparatus & Setup

Crucial Setup Rules:
1. Center Groove: The ruler's groove keeps the marble rolling in a straight, repeatable line.
2. Release Height: Always release the marble from rest level with the top book of the stack. Never push it!
3. Constant Speed Zone: Start your stopwatch the instant the marble hits the flat table (0 cm), and stop it when it crosses your finish line.

Step 3: Calculate Average Time & Speed across Table

1. Average Time: Add your 3 times together, then divide by 3.
2. Speed across Table: Divide your marked table distance by your average time (v = d / t_avg).

📈 Step 4: Draw Position vs. Time Graph (x vs. t)

Draw your Position vs. Time motion graph by clicking to create datapoints! The slope of each line represents the marble's speed across the table.

💡 Kinematic Slope Discovery:
• Slope = Rise / Run: The vertical rise is distance (Δx) and the horizontal run is time (Δt).
• Steepness = Speed: Notice how the High Ramp produces a steeper slope because the marble travels the same distance in fewer seconds!

⚡ Step 5: Draw Velocity vs. Time Graph (v vs. t)

Because we assume the marble rolls across the flat table at a constant speed, its velocity does not change! Plot the start and end velocity points to create horizontal lines.

💡 Area Under the Curve Discovery:
• Flat Horizontal Line: On a velocity vs. time graph, a flat line means constant, unchanging speed.
• Geometric Area = Distance: The rectangular shaded area equals (base × height) = (t_avg × v) = distance traveled across the table!
