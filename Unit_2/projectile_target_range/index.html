Step 2 of 9

Fold the Cardstock into a 3-Sided Channel

Fold each 11-inch strip the narrow way into a U-shaped channel with 3 equal sides, then overlap the channels like roof shingles and tape them together so the ramp is rigid and strong.

A
Fold in Thirds the NARROW Way (Lengthwise — Keep It 11 Inches Long!)

Each strip is 11 in long and 2.83 in wide. Fold two creases running the entire 11-inch length so the narrow 2.83 in width is divided into 3 equal strips (~0.94 in each). Fold both long sides up at 90° to make a long 11-inch U-channel.

B
Overlap Channels Like Roof Shingles (Do NOT Tape End-to-End!)

Taping end-to-end is too flexible and floppy! Instead, nest and overlap each channel by several inches like shingles on a roof (with the upper channel sitting inside/on top of the lower channel so the marble rolls smoothly downhill without hitting a lip) and tape tightly.

Step 3 of 9 • 🛑 Teacher Checkpoint #1

Curve the Bottom End of Your Ramp

Pick an end to be the TOP and an end to be the BOTTOM of the ramp. Cut the side walls of the cardstock at the bottom of the ramp and tape it into a gentle curve shape.

1
Snip the Side Walls Near the Bottom End

Make 3 or 4 small vertical scissor cuts in both side walls near the bottom end of your ramp (stop at the fold line—do NOT cut through the bottom floor of the channel!).

2
Overlap the Flaps & Tape Into a Gentle Curve

Bend the bottom of the ramp slightly upward so it transitions smoothly from a steep slope to flat, overlapping the cut side flaps and taping them in place.

✂️ Cut slits in both side walls up to the dashed fold line, then overlap and tape the tabs to form a smooth curve.

Step 4 of 9

Put a Chair on the Table

Lift one lab chair and place it stably on top of your lab table. This chair will hold up the high end of your marble ramp.

✓
All 4 Chair Legs Fully on the Tabletop

Place the chair about 1 meter back from the front edge of your table so all four chair legs sit flat and securely on the table surface.

✓
Clear Runway to the Table Edge

Make sure the tabletop path between the chair and the edge of the table is completely clear of backpacks, Chromebooks, and water bottles.

Step 5 of 9

Attach the Ramp to the Chair & Table Edge

Attach the top of the ramp to the chair and position the bottom end of the ramp so the marble will roll horizontally off the edge of the table and NOT be launched at an angle directly from the ramp.

1
Tape Top of Ramp to the Chair

Secure the top end of your cardstock channel high up on the chair using masking tape so it cannot slip or sag between trials.

2
Flat Horizontal Exit at the Table Edge

Tape the curved bottom of the ramp flat onto the tabletop right near the edge so the marble rolls flat along the table before leaving the edge horizontally.

Step 6 of 9 • 🛑 Teacher Checkpoint #2

Place & Tape a Meter Stick on the Floor

Place 1 meter stick flat on the floor starting directly below the edge of the table (0 cm at the table edge) and tape it to the floor so it doesn't accidentally slide around.

1
Line Up 0 cm Directly Under the Table Edge

Use your second meter stick held vertically against the table edge as a plumb line so the 0 cm end of the floor meter stick sits directly beneath the launch edge.

2
Tape the Meter Stick to the Floor

Point the meter stick straight out along the marble's path and tape both ends to the floor so no one kicks or shifts it during your trials.

🛑
Pause Here: Approval Required from Mr. Mudry

Step 7 of 9

Mark the Length of Your Ramp Every 10 cm

Using your second meter stick, measure along the length of your ramp and draw a clear line every 10 cm from the bottom of the ramp to the top (10 cm, 20 cm, 30 cm, 40 cm, 50 cm, 60 cm...).

1
Start 0 cm at the Bottom of the Ramp

Hold your second meter stick right alongside the cardstock channel with 0 cm at the bottom where the ramp meets the table.

2
Draw & Label Lines Every 10 cm Up the Ramp

Draw a clear line across the inside of the channel at 10 cm, 20 cm, 30 cm, 40 cm, 50 cm, 60 cm (all the way to the top of your ramp) and write the number next to each line.

Step 8 of 9

Complete Your Calibration Data Table

Release the marble from each 10 cm mark on your ramp. Have a partner watch closely where the marble first hits the floor next to your taped meter stick, and record the exact distance along the meter stick (cm).
