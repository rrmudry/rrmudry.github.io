Part 2: Calculating Rate of Change & Making a Prediction (2 Points) Stacked Math Practice
(3A) Worked Pattern: Rate of Graph 1
Every +1 m/s of horizontal speed adds +3 m of distance because the flight time is 3 s:
t = Δx ÷ vox
t = 30 m ÷ 10 m/s = 3 s
(3B) Your Turn: Predict Trial 6 (vox = 25 m/s)
If students launch a ball at vox = 25 m/s with the same flight time (t = 3 s), how far will it land?
Δx = vox × t
Δx = m/s × 3 s = m

☐ ZONE B — EVIDENCE (2 pts): Fill in two data points from Table 2 (include numbers and units!).
• Data Point 1: At a launch angle of 45°, the hang time is 2.8 s and the landing range reaches a maximum of m.
• Data Point 2: When the angle is increased to 60° (or 75°), the hang time increases to s, but the landing range drops down to m.
☐ ZONE C — REASONING (1 pt): Connect the evidence to the physics formula Δx = vox × thang.
Because horizontal landing range equals Horizontal Speed (vox) × Hang Time (thang), shooting at a very steep angle gives the ball more time in the air, but leaves it with too little horizontal (vox drops to only 10.0 m/s at 60° and 5.2 m/s at 75°).
