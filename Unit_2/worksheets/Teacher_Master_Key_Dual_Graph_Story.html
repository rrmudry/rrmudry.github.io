Teacher Grading Guide • 10 Points
Criterion Points Mastery Indicators (Evidence of Full Credit)
1. Scenario & Narrative 2 pts Original 3-stage story across 10.0s with clear object, reference origin (x=0m), realistic positions, and distinct stages (forward, stopped, or reverse).
2. 3-Stage Motion Table 3 pts All 3 time intervals sum to 10.0s; correct signs on displacements (Δx); step-by-step GUESS velocity formulas (v = Δx/Δt) with correct units (m/s).
3. Graph 1 (x-t) & Slopes 2 pts 3 continuous connected line segments plotted accurately; slope calculations (Rise/Run) explicitly shown below graph matching table velocities.
4. Graph 2 (v-t) & Areas 2 pts 3 horizontal velocity bars drawn at correct heights; shaded rectangular regions between bars and v=0 axis; Area = v × Δt proves displacement Δx.
5. Dual-Graph Synthesis 1 pt Sum of shaded areas matches net displacement (x_final − x_initial); correct physical meaning of negative slope/velocity; unit cancellation shown.
Worked Exemplar (Mars Rover Telemetry): Rover starts at x = 14m. Section 1 (0-3s): Reverses at -4 m/s to x = 2m (Δx = -12m). Section 2 (3-6s): Stops for 3s to analyze rock sample (Δx = 0m). Section 3 (6-10s): Drives forward for 4s at +3 m/s back to x = 14m (Δx = +12m). Net Δx = 0m, Total Distance = 24m.
Mathematical Verification • Slope = Velocity & Area = Displacement Dual-Graph Alignment Proof
Section 1 (0s to 3s):
• x-t Slope: (-12m − 0m) / (3s) = -4.0 m/s
• v-t Area: (-4 m/s) × (3 s) = -12.0 m
Negative slope → negative velocity bar below axis!
Section 2 (3s to 6s):
• x-t Slope: (0m) / (3s) = 0.0 m/s
• v-t Area: (0 m/s) × (3 s) = 0.0 m
Flat horizontal position line → line rests on v=0 axis!
Section 3 (6s to 10s) & Total:
• x-t Slope: (+12m) / (4s) = +3.0 m/s
• v-t Area: (+3 m/s) × (4 s) = +12.0 m
• Net Displacement: ∑Area = -12m + 0m + 12m = 0.0 m
Matches Graph 1: x_end (14m) − x_start (14m) = 0 m!
