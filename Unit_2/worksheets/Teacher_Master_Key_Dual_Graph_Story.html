TEACHER REFERENCE KEY
📊 10-Point Holistic Grading Rubric:
1. Illustration (2 pts): Clear moving object, origin (0,0) labeled, motion direction vector arrow.
2. Word Problem (2 pts): Authentic constant-speed story stating 2 knowns with units and 1 clear target unknown.
3. GUESS Proof (3 pts): G, U, E, S, S completely filled out, algebra correct, final answer boxed with units.
4. Dual Graphs (3 pts): Calibrated axes, straight line on x-t (slope = v), horizontal line on v-t (area = d).
🌟 Complete Student Work Exemplar: "The Mars Rover Traverse"

Student Story / Problem:
"NASA's autonomous Mars Recon Rover drives across the flat plains of Jezero Crater at a steady speed of 15.0 m/s for a total time of 40.0 seconds. How many meters of Martian terrain does the rover cross before its next sensor scan?"

Complete GUESS Mathematical Proof:

GSpeed v = 15.0 m/s • Time t = 40.0 s
UDistance d = ? meters
Ed = v • t
Sd = (15.0 m/s) • (40.0 s)
Sd = 600 meters [Boxed]

Scale Planning Choices:

  • Time (Horizontal): 10 blocks = 50 s (5 s per block). Point plotted at t = 40 s (8th line).
  • Position x (Graph 1): 8 blocks = 800 m (100 m per block). Plotted from (0, 0) to (40, 600).
  • Velocity v (Graph 2): 6 blocks = 30 m/s (5 m/s per block). Horizontal line at v = 15 m/s (3rd line).
Synthesis Answers:
1. Slope = velocity / speed (600m / 40s = 15 m/s).
2. Area = distance / displacement (15 m/s × 40 s = 600 m).
3. If 2× speed: Slope doubles (steeper line); Area doubles (box is twice as tall = 1,200 m).
Graph 1: x vs. t Visual Check
• Line MUST be straight (not curved).
• Starts at origin (0, 0) and ends at (40 s, 600 m).
• Slope = Rise / Run = 600 / 40 = 15.0 m/s.
Graph 2: v vs. t Visual Check
• Line MUST be perfectly horizontal at height v = 15 m/s.
• Extends from t = 0 to t = 40 s.
• Shaded Rectangle Area = 15 m/s × 40 s = 600 m.
Common Student Pitfalls to Watch For:
• Curved Lines: Students drawing curves instead of straight lines (constant speed = linear x-t, horizontal v-t).
• Slanted v-t line: Students drawing a sloped line on v-t (remind: sloped v-t means accelerating!).
• Unit Dropping: Forgetting units on Givens or final answer (e.g. writing "600" instead of "600 m").
• Misaligned Time Scale: Using different time scales on Graph 1 and Graph 2. They should align!
