📐 Step-by-Step Fabrication Protocol & Physical Tolerances Engineering Design Process
Step 1: Fuselage Rolling & Tolerance Fit [Tuesday Build]
Wrap cardstock tightly around the 1/2-inch PVC rolling mandrel. Secure longitudinal seam with 3 thin pieces of clear tape. Slide tube back and forth to verify smooth travel without friction binding.
✓ Gravity Drop Test: Tube must slide freely off vertical mandrel under its own weight.
Step 2: Fin Geometry & Symmetry [Tuesday Build]
Cut 3 or 4 identical cardstock fins using the precision template. Crease 5 mm mounting tabs. Align fins parallel to fuselage centerline spaced at 120° (3 fins) or 90° (4 fins).
✓ Symmetry Check: Fins must be rigidly perpendicular (no twists that induce barrel roll).
Step 3: Nose Cone & Clay Ballast [Wednesday QC]
Roll cardstock into a smooth aerodynamic nose cone. Pack a calibrated 5–10 g lump of modeling clay firmly into the tip. Secure nose cone to fuselage with an airtight tape wrap.
✓ Airtight Seal Check: Gently blow into fuselage base; zero air should leak through nose cone.
Step 4: String-Loop Balance Test [Wednesday QC]
Tie a slip-knot string loop around the rocket. Adjust loop until rocket balances horizontally. Mark this physical Center of Mass (CM). Verify CM is 1–2 tube diameters forward of fins.
✓ Swing Test: Spin balanced rocket overhead on 1m string; it must fly nose-forward, not tumble.

🧮 Quantitative Flight Kinematics Calculations (Friday Analysis) g = 10 m/s² Downward
1. Time to Apogee (tup) tup = thang / 2
Flight symmetry: ascent time equals descent time.
2. Launch Exit Velocity (v₀) v₀ = 10 · tup
At apogee v = 0, so 0 = v₀ - gtup ➔ v₀ = 10 · tup (m/s).
3. Maximum Apogee Altitude (hmax) hmax = 5 · (tup)²
Free-fall displacement: h = ½gt² = 5(tup)² (meters). Convert to ft (×3.28).
4. Launch Tube Acceleration (atube) atube = v₀² / (2 · L)
Tube length L = 0.40 m. Use v₀² = 2aL ➔ atube = v₀² / 0.80 (m/s²).
