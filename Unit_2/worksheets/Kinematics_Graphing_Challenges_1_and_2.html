A delivery truck drives along a straight highway in three distinct parts starting from the station (x = 0 m):

• Part 3 (50 to 90 s): Resumes driving at 25 m/s → Reaches x = 1,300 m at t = 90 s.

Graph Analysis Questions Use your graph and formulas to answer
1. Understanding the Rest Stop (Flat Line):
What does the completely flat horizontal line between t = 20 s and t = 50 s tell you about the truck's motion? How do you know the truck is NOT moving?
2. Calculating Patrol Car Speeds (Speed = Distance / Time):
• Option A (Catch at rest stop): Car travels 300 m in 20 seconds (from 30 s to 50 s).
  Speed = 300 m ÷ 20 s = m/s
• Option B (Catch at end): Car travels 1,300 m in 60 seconds (from 30 s to 90 s).
  Speed = 1,300 m ÷ 60 s = m/s
