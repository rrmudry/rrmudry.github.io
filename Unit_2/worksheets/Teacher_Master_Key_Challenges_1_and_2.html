Challenge 1: Harbor Boat & Supply Drone RendezvousPage 1 of Student Sheet

Plotted Points on Grid:
• Boat: (0s, 300m), (10s, 360m), (20s, 420m), (30s, 480m), (40s, 540m), (50s, 600m), (55s, 630m), (60s, 660m).
• Drone: (0 to 20s flat on dock at 0m), (30s, 180m), (40s, 360m), (50s, 540m), (55s, 630m), (60s, 720m).

1. Rendezvous Coordinates: Time t = 55 seconds • Position x = 630 meters from dock.
2. Steepness & Speed: The Drone line is steeper. A steeper slope means a faster speed (covers more meters each second).
3. Actual Drone Flight Duration: 55 s − 20 s delay = 35 seconds in the air.
4. Battery Range Check: Round-trip distance = 630 m out + 630 m back = 1,260 meters.
→ Since max battery range is 1,200 m, No, the drone cannot make it back (crashes 60 m short of the dock).
Challenge 2: Highway Courier Journey & Patrol InterceptPage 2 of Student Sheet

Plotted Points on Grid:
• Delivery Truck: (0s, 0m) → (20s, 300m) → (50s, 300m, flat line) → (90s, 1,300m).
• Patrol Line A: Starts at (30s, 0m), connects to rest stop at (50s, 300m).
• Patrol Line B: Starts at (30s, 0m), connects to highway end at (90s, 1,300m).

1. Understanding Rest Stop: The position stays at 300 m while time continues to tick forward (slope = 0 m/s). This proves the truck is stationary (stopped).
2. Patrol Car Speed Calculations:
• Option A (Rest Stop at t = 50 s): Speed = 300 m / 20 s = 15 m/s.
• Option B (Highway End at t = 90 s): Speed = 1,300 m / 60 s = 21.7 m/s.
3. Patrol Safe Speed Limit (Max Limit = 20 m/s):
• Option A needs 15 m/s (15 ≤ 20 m/s) → SAFE & POSSIBLE ✓
• Option B needs 21.7 m/s (21.7 > 20 m/s) → EXCEEDS SPEED LIMIT ✗
→ The patrol car must choose Option A to intercept at the rest stop.
