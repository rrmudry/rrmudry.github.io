☐ (1B) Calculate Slope of Platform B Line (●)
From vox = 0 to 20 m/s:  Slope = ΔxB ÷ vox

Part 2: Completing Missing 60° Table Data Using Trig & Range Formula (3 Points) Given: cos(60°) = 0.50  |  sin(60°) = 0.866
☐ (2A) Find Velocity Components at θ = 60° (vo = 20 m/s)
1. Horizontal Speed (vox = vo cos 60°):
   vox = 20 m/s × 0.50 = m/s
2. Initial Vertical Speed (voy = vo sin 60°):
   voy = 20 m/s × 0.866 = m/s
☐ (2B) Calculate Missing Landing Range at θ = 60°
Use your vox from (2A) and thang = 3.46 s from Table 2:
Δx = vox × thang
Δx = m/s × 3.46 s = m
