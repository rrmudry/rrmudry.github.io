1. PROJECTILE MOTION VOCABULARY & FORMULA TOOLKIT (g = 10 m/s²) Use These Terms in Your Reasoning
Horizontal Speed (vox) Initial Vertical Speed (voy) Hang Time (thang) Landing Range (Δx) Linear Trend (Constant Slope) Complementary Angles (Add to 90°) Optimal Angle (45°)
• Horizontal Range Formula:
Δx = vox × t
• Slope of Range vs. Speed Graph:
Slope = Δx ÷ vox = Flight Time (t)
• Level-Ground Hang Time:
thang = 2voy ÷ g
2. ZONE A — CLAIM SENTENCE STARTERS (1 Point) Directly Answer the Question & State the Overall Pattern
Option 1 (Evaluating a Student Misconception):
“The student's claim is incorrect because increasing the launch angle past 45° causes the horizontal landing range (Δx) to decrease, not increase.”
Option 2 (Comparing Complementary Angles like 30° and 60°):
“The student's argument is incorrect because launch angles of 30° and 60° are complementary angles (adding to 90°) that produce the exact same horizontal landing range of _____ m on level ground.”
Option 3 (Describing Graph Trends):
“As [ independent variable ] increases from _____ to _____, the [ dependent variable ] increases linearly / reaches a maximum at 45° and then decreases symmetrically.”
3. ZONE B — QUANTITATIVE EVIDENCE SENTENCE STARTERS (2 Points) Cite At Least TWO Data Points + Units!
Comparing Peak Angle (45°) vs. Steep Angles (60° or 75°):
• “According to Table 2 and Graph 2, at a launch angle of 45°, the hang time is 2.8 s and the projectile reaches its maximum landing range of 40 m.”
• “In contrast, when the launch angle increases to 60° (or 75°), the hang time increases to 3.5 s (or 3.9 s), but the landing range drops to 35 m (or 20 m).”
Comparing Complementary Angles (30° vs. 60°):
• “In Table 2, a 30° launch has a fast horizontal speed (vox = 17.3 m/s) and a shorter hang time (thang = 2.00 s), landing at 34.6 m.”
• “Meanwhile, a 60° launch has a slower horizontal speed (vox = 10.0 m/s) and a longer hang time (thang = 3.46 s), landing at the exact same range of 34.6 m.”
4. ZONE C — PHYSICS REASONING SENTENCE STARTERS (1 Point) Explain WHY Using the Equation Δx = vox × thang
Explaining the Trade-Off Between Horizontal Speed and Hang Time:
“Because horizontal landing range is determined by multiplying Horizontal Speed × Hang Time (Δx = vox × thang), launching at a very steep angle gives the ball more vertical hang time, but leaves it with too little horizontal speed (vox) to travel far forward.”
Explaining Why 30° & 60° Tie and Why 45° Wins:
“For complementary angles like 30° and 60°, the horizontal and vertical velocity components trade places, so the product vox × thang equals 34.6 m for both. A 45° launch maximizes range (40.0 m) because it splits launch speed equally between horizontal speed and vertical hang time.”
