PHASE 1 SELECT YOUR INERTIA REGIME & CODENAME
1. Inertia of Rest
Stationary mass stubbornly stays at rest when support is pulled fast (e.g. Tablecloth slip, coin-card flick, embroidery hoop drop).
2. Inertia of Motion
Moving mass stubbornly continues at constant velocity when carrier halts (e.g. Cart barrier crash, curb stop, rolling launch).
3. Inertia of Direction
Rotating mass flies off along straight tangent line when force is cut (e.g. Whirling string cut, pie-pan exit gap, wheel throw).
