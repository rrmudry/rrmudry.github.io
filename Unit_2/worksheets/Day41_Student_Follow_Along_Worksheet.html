Part 5: The 2-Column Problem Solving Method
Golden Rule: Never Mix X and Y Numbers
HORIZONTAL MOTION (X-AXIS) VERTICAL MOTION (Y-AXIS)
• Motion Type: Constant Velocity
• Horizontal Acceleration: ax = 0 m/s²
• Velocity Formula: vx = constant
• Distance Formula: Δx = vx · t 		• Motion Type: Free Fall Accelerated by Gravity
• Gravitational Acceleration: g = 10 m/s² (down)
• Initial Vertical Velocity: v0y = 0 m/s (starts from rest)
• Fall Distance Formula: Δy = 5 · t²  (or t = √(Δy / 5))
🔗 THE BRIDGE: Time (t) is the ONLY variable shared between the horizontal and vertical sides!
