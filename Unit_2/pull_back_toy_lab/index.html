Stage 3 of 5 • CRITICAL TECHNIQUE

Start Stopwatch BEFORE Car Is Released!

Prop up a second device with this stopwatch (or click ⛶ Fullscreen Timer) in the exact camera shot. Have the timer running before release! It is impossible to start a timer at the exact millisecond of release, so letting it run first provides precise video timestamps.

Pull Back 2–3 Clicks & Smooth Release

Pull back 2–3 clicks maximum (do NOT push down hard on the axles). Hold front bumper motionless at x = 0.0 cm (v₀ = 0) and release cleanly without pushing. Record until car crosses past 100 cm.

🧮 Step 4: Kinematic Calculations & Verification

Productive Science Practice
Calculation 1

Average Speed of the Car (v_avg)

v_avg = Δx / Δt

Calculate the average speed of the car over the entire 100 cm track: v_avg = 100 cm / t_total

v_avg = cm/s
Calculation 2

Final Speed at 1.0 Meter (v_f)

v_avg = (v₀ + v_f) / 2

Because the car accelerated uniformly from rest, initial velocity v₀ = 0 cm/s. Therefore, v_f = 2 · v_avg.

v_f = cm/s
Calculation 3

Acceleration of the Pull-Back Car (a)

v_f = v₀ + a · t

Use your final velocity and total elapsed time to calculate the uniform acceleration: a = (v_f - v₀) / t_total = v_f / t_total

a = cm/s²
