Stage 3 of 5 • CRITICAL TECHNIQUE

Prop Up Stopwatch & Start Running BEFORE Release

Place your second device (propped against a pencil case, water bottle, or stand) facing the camera right beside the track. Click ⛶ Fullscreen Timer for massive, high-visibility digits.

⚡ Why Start Before Release? It is impossible to release a car and tap "Start" at the exact same millisecond. Starting the clock several seconds ahead ensures the timer is already ticking smoothly in your camera shot, giving you an exact, zeroable Start Time (t₀) when the car first moves!

🚗
Stage 4 of 5 • Release Execution

Pull Back 2–3 Clicks & Smooth Release

Pull back 2–3 clicks maximum (do NOT push down hard on the axles). Hold front bumper motionless at x = 0.0 cm (v₀ = 0) and release cleanly without pushing. Record until car crosses past 100 cm.

🧮 Step 4: Kinematic Calculations & Verification

Productive Science Practice
Calculation 1

Average Speed of the Car (vavg)

vavg = Δx / Δt

Calculate the average speed of the car over the entire 100 cm track: vavg = 100 cm / ttotal

vavg = cm/s
Calculation 2

Final Speed at 1.0 Meter (vf)

vavg = (v₀ + vf) / 2

Because the car accelerated uniformly from rest, initial velocity v₀ = 0 cm/s. Therefore, vf = 2 · vavg.

vf = cm/s
Calculation 3

Acceleration of the Pull-Back Car (a)

vf = v₀ + a · t

Use your final velocity and total elapsed time to calculate the uniform acceleration: a = (vf − v₀) / ttotal = vf / ttotal

a = cm/s²
