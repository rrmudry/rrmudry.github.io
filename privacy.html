Student Privacy & Data Protection

Protected under Google Cloud Platform and Google Workspace for Education. Simple, safe, and built strictly for classroom learning.

Protected by the Google Education Security Umbrella

All student work, lab results, and class rosters are stored directly within Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Google Workspace for Education under the instructor's verified district account (@orangeusd.org). Student records are guarded by Google’s enterprise educational security standards and federal FERPA privacy regulations. No third-party servers or commercial platforms ever hold student data.

District Google Cloud

Everything is tied directly to your school district Google account. Data is encrypted using Google’s enterprise-grade encryption at rest and in transit.

Classroom Use Only

Data is used solely for standard high school science instruction—such as saving a physics lab simulation, answering a bell-ringer, or receiving assignment scores.

No Ads & No Selling

We will never sell student information, run commercial advertisements, track personal browsing habits, or share data with outside companies or marketers.

Plain-English Privacy Commitments

What Information Is Used?

Only the standard educational credentials already provided by your school: your student name, 6-digit school ID, district email (@orangeusd.org), and your physics lab/quiz responses so you can see your work and receive feedback.

What Is Never Collected?

We never ask for or store home addresses, phone numbers, parent financial info, Social Security numbers, tracking cookies, or personal device data.

FERPA & Student Rights

All records comply with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and California student privacy laws. Students and parents have the right to inspect, review, or request corrections to their educational records at any time.
