Student Data Privacy & Records Policy

A transparent, plain-English overview of student data protection, federal & California privacy compliance, and secure classroom technologies.

Our Privacy Pledge to Students & Families

Mr. Mudry’s Physics instructional website (rrmudry.github.io) and its associated interactive laboratory tools are designed from the ground up to uphold the highest standards of student privacy. We operate on a strict zero-commercial-tracking policy. We never sell, market, monetize, or profile student data, and collect only the bare minimum information necessary for classroom science instruction.

FERPA Compliance

Complies with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (34 CFR Part 99). All educational records and assignment submissions remain under the direct control of the classroom teacher and school district, accessed solely under legitimate educational interests.

California SOPIPA & COPPA

Adheres to California’s Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (SOPIPA / SB 1177), AB 1584, and the federal Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). No behavioral advertising, profiling, or unauthorized data retention is conducted.

Google Cloud Security

Data is stored in an encrypted Google Cloud Platform Firestore database (site-6e500) bound to the instructor's verified district account (@orangeusd.org). All data in transit uses TLS/HTTPS encryption, and data at rest is protected with AES-256.

Detailed Data Practices

1. Google Classroom Integration & Sync

Our local synchronization service connects via Google Classroom APIs using instructor-authorized OAuth tokens. It allows the teacher to pull active course rosters and push grades directly back to the district’s Google Classroom gradebook. Roster synchronization occurs only under teacher direction, without third-party intermediary access.

2. Third-Party Sharing & Commercial Prohibition

We strictly maintain a no-commercialization guarantee:

  • Student records are never sold, rented, or leased to third parties or data brokers.
  • The site contains no third-party commercial advertisements, marketing scripts, or affiliate links.
  • No analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or social media tracking pixels) are installed on student-facing pages.

3. Data Retention & Annual Deletion Schedule

Student roster information and active assessment sessions are maintained strictly for the duration of the active academic school year. At the conclusion of each school year, previous rosters are purged from active database collections to ensure student data is not retained beyond its educational utility.

4. Student, Parent & Guardian Rights

In accordance with FERPA and California Education Code:

  • Parents, legal guardians, and eligible students have the right to inspect and review all educational records stored within this system.
  • Parents or guardians may request correction or deletion of erroneous personal information by contacting the instructor or school administration.
  • Students can review their own submitted lab metrics, bell-ringer scores, and completed assessments via their student dashboard using their district ID.
