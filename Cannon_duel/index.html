How to play

  1. Set your launch angle θ and launch speed v0 with the sliders, the ± buttons, the arrow keys, or by typing exact numbers.
  2. Press FIRE! (or Space). Each hit knocks down the other fort's health. First to 0 loses.
  3. Missed? The flag tells you how many meters short or long you landed, and a faded path shows your last shots.

The physics

  • g = 10 m/s², no air resistance. The ball launches from the center (pivot) of your cannon.
  • Recon (and the 📐 button) gives Δx and Δy from your pivot to the enemy's pivot.
  • Every ammo type follows the same path for the same θ and v0: mass doesn't change a projectile's trajectory.
  • Want a first-shot hit? Use x = v0cosθ · t and y = v0sinθ · t − ½gt².

Keys: ←/→ angle, ↑/↓ speed (hold Shift for 0.1 steps), Space to fire.
