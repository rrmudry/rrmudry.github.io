About the Physics

An object in free fall speeds up at a constant rate: the acceleration due to gravity. Down is negative, so on Earth a = −10 m/s². Every object is dropped from rest (vo = 0). Without air resistance, the object's mass does not change how it falls. Astronaut David Scott showed this on the Moon in 1971 by dropping a hammer and a feather together. They hit the ground at the same time.

Position

Used to find the time to fall (xf = 0 at the ground).

xf = xo + vot + (1/2)at2

Velocity

Used to find the final velocity. It is the slope of the position graph.

vf = vo + at
