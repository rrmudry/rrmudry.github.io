Horizontal Projectile Motion Explorer

An interactive tool to explore the physics of projectiles.

Controls & Inputs

Welcome to the projectile motion explorer. This tool, based on the physics principles from the report, lets you simulate horizontal launches. Choose a calculation mode, adjust the inputs using the sliders, and see the results and trajectory update instantly.

Results & Visualization

This panel displays the calculated outcomes of your simulation. Below the key metrics, a to-scale scene shows the projectile's path from the cliff edge (0, xo) to the ground. The scale never changes, so you can compare launches directly.

Time in Air (t) -- s
Horizontal Range (xrange) -- m
Initial H. Velocity (vox) -- m/s
Final Vertical Velocity (vfy) -- m/s

    Dots are 0.5 s apart. Ground ticks show horizontal position; cliff ticks show height. Faded paths are your last 3 launches.

    About the Physics

    The calculations in this simulator are governed by the fundamental principle of projectile motion: horizontal and vertical motion are independent. We can analyze them separately using a constant vertical acceleration (a = -10 m/s²) and zero horizontal acceleration.

    Vertical Motion

    Used to find the time in the air. The initial vertical velocity (vo) is 0.

    xf = xo + vot + (1/2)at2

    Horizontal Motion

    Used to find the range or velocity.

    v = xrange/t
