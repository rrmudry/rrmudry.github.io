CHOOSE YOUR OPPONENT

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Win a small board with 3 in a row to capture it. Capture 3 small boards in a row to win the war.
  2. Where you play sends your opponent. Play the top-right square of any board → they must play in the top-right board.
  3. Sent to a board that's already finished? That's a FREE MOVE: play anywhere that's still open.

Tip: the glowing board is where you must play. Stuck? Hit HINT. The bar above the board shows who the AI thinks is winning.
