AI Mentor & Student Privacy

How we harness artificial intelligence to support physics inquiry while fiercely guarding your student's personal data and privacy.

🛡️

The Core Guarantee

Zero student personal information (no names, no emails, no student IDs) is ever transmitted to the AI. All educational records stay strictly within our protected school database.

🚫 👤

1. 100% Anonymized

When students chat with the physics mentor, the AI only receives the physics dialogue. Names, school emails, and student ID numbers (PII) are completely stripped beforehand.

ZERO PERSONAL INFO (PII) SHARED
🔒 📑

2. No Model Training

Unlike public consumer tools, our classroom connects via enterprise Google Cloud APIs. Student inquiries are never used to train future AI models and are never sold or shared.

COMMERCIAL PRIVACY TERMS
👀 👨‍🏫

3. 100% Teacher Supervised

I have a live classroom dashboard open during every warm-up. Every student's interaction is recorded in our school database, allowing me to view transcripts and assist in real time.

LIVE DASHBOARD OVERSIGHT
🎯 ⏱️

4. Strict Guardrails

The AI is locked into a supportive physics tutor persona. It cannot chat about non-academic topics, will not give away test answers, and sessions are capped at 3–5 minutes.

PEDAGOGICAL GUARDRAILS

What Information Leaves the Classroom?

❌ What the AI NEVER Sees

  • 🚫 Student Full Name
  • 🚫 School District Email / Login
  • 🚫 Student ID Number
  • 🚫 Grades & Academic Records
  • 🚫 Physical Location / Device IP

✅ What the AI DOES See

  • ✔️ Today's Physics Prompt (e.g. "What keeps a satellite in orbit?")
  • ✔️ Student's Science Thinking & Explanations
  • ✔️ Context Needed to Offer Hints & Socratic Encouragement
🏛️

Federal Student Privacy Standards

FERPA

Family Educational Rights & Privacy Act

Protects student educational records. Because no educational records or student identities are ever disclosed to the generative AI service, and all student grades remain strictly within our secured school database, our system is fully FERPA-compliant.

COPPA

Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act

Prevents commercial data collection from minors. The AI mentor operates under strict enterprise API terms: no student profiling, zero targeted advertisements, and no retention or reuse of student inquiries for model training.
