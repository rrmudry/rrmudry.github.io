Accuracy Target Proximity

How close a measurement (or your emoji painting) is to the true, intended reference design (correct facial features, proportions, and colors).

Precision Clustering / Repeatability

How close repeated measurements or marks are to each other (consistency, tight grouping, and instrument repeatability), regardless of whether they hit the bullseye.
