🧊 How to Play & 3D Winning Lines

Welcome to Epic 3D Tic-Tac-Toe 2077! Unlike flat 2D tic-tac-toe, the game takes place inside a 3-dimensional cube of cells (3×3×3 or 4×4×4 Qubic).

1. Orthogonal Lines

Straight lines running horizontally along rows, vertically down columns, or vertically like pillars straight through the floors.

27 lines in 3³, 48 in 4³

2. Planar Diagonals

2D diagonals lying on any horizontal floor, vertical front/back wall, or side vertical wall.

18 lines in 3³, 24 in 4³

3. Space Diagonals

True 3D diagonals that cut directly through the 3D cube from one outer corner to the exact opposite outer corner.

4 lines in both

Why 4×4×4 Qubic is the Grandmaster Standard

In standard 3×3×3, 13 winning lines pass through the exact center cell, giving the first player an overwhelming advantage.

In 4×4×4 Qubic, there are 64 cells and 76 winning lines. No single cell is in the absolute center! Every corner and every internal cell belongs to exactly 7 winning lines, creating chess-like tactical depth, multiple forks, and unblockable combination attacks.

Pro-Tips

  • Use the Exploded Layer Slices to quickly scan cross-floor diagonals that might be hidden in 3D.
  • Turn on Threat Radar to see real-time alert borders on any line with 2-in-a-row (3³) or 3-in-a-row (4³).
  • Adjust the Layer Gap slider to separate the floating decks if lower levels are occluded.
